How It Works

3 Easy Steps to a Clean Alandi

Civic waste management simplified. No paperwork, no endless waiting.

STEP 01

Snap & Report

Spot an overflowing bin or roadside dumping? Click "Report Waste" or click on the Live Map to capture location and photo in 15 seconds.

STEP 02

Ward Team Dispatched

Your complaint instantly pings the dedicated sanitation supervisor for that ward. Pickup route is updated dynamically.

STEP 03

Track & Verify

Watch status move to "In Progress" and "Resolved" in real-time on the map and your personal citizen dashboard.

Quick Suggestions:

Green Bin — Biodegradable & Wet

Kitchen food scraps, fruit/vegetable peels, leaves, sacred floral Nirmalya for organic composting.

Blue Bin — Dry Recyclable

Paper, flattened cartons, plastic bottles, metal cans, glass jars, dry clean containers.

Red Bin — Domestic Hazardous & E-Waste

Batteries, expired medicines, broken fluorescent bulbs, syringes, electronic cables.
